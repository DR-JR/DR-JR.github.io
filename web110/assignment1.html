Ryne McCormick While most of the links in the activities section of the Lesson 1 wiki article were broken or dead web pages, some of the ones that did work contained some great information. While I would consider myself an advanced user of computers, I am always open to learning new things. One of these is how to use the command Traceroute. I never knew there was a powershell command to backtrack your connection to a website and see the progress and hops along the way. This will be very useful in diagnosing internet issues in the future, and connectivity problems between both internal and external networks. It was cool to find a use for a computer's Ping that isn't just an indication of how stable your connection is to counter-strike.
