Raster Images

Raster Images are a common way to display and compress images online, where each pixel corresponds to an exact color and the whole field is constantly refreshed. This is the same concept as an old CRT monitor and I miss degaussing the magnetic field on those.

Vector Images

Vector images take up much more data than a raster image because they hold their integrity at larger scales. Zooming into a raster image will leave the image blurry and pixelated, but zooming into a vector image should still be clear and sharp. This is best used for logos or simple images with color and shape for best effect.
