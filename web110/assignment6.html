Ryne McCormick 1) Internet Services When I think of the internet, I don't really think of it as four separate things (communication services, information retrieval services, web services, world wide web) I just think of it as this amorphous digital environment where all of these things exist as a part of a larger tapestry. Communication services like email, irc, voip, telnet, and messaging are used by nearly everybody everyday across the planet. Information retrieval services are not used by most people every day, FTP being dropped by browsers recently due to security issues. Web services allow apps to interact, and the world wide web is what people probably mainly think of when they think about the internet. Maybe that's changed in the last 20 years with smartphones and tiktok, but in my mind the world wide web is the default mental image of the internet. 2) VoIP / Messaging There is a set of netiquette rules or suggestions for online chatting that people generally try to follow to remain civil while they are basically anonymous. Usually proper grammar and spelling is encouraged, along with not overusing CAPS LOCK, and generally being pleasant and not aggressive. Any other rules in the chat should obviously be followed, as well as the ones determined by the service hosting the chats. WhatsApp is my favorite of these, as it lets people connect internationally and securely without ridiculous fees. It is great for hosting video chats, group chats for people who are in many different locations, and posting updates for groups or individuals. The security features of WhatsApp were the most attractive to me as the messages and calls are end-to-end encrypted, meaning only those in the conversation can see it. 3) Screen Sharing / Remote Desktop - Chrome Remote Desktop Chrome remote desktop has come a long way from being a web browser extension and is even available through cell phones now. While I am sure the uses for the remote desktop software are normally for work access or troubleshooting IT, I have found the mose use of this software in playing tabletop RPGs. It is useful to remote connect to my computer with a tablet to call up character sheets when I am away from home. I have also personally found it immensely useful when I was recovering from hernia surgery twice as sitting up in bed was quite difficult, so controlling my computer from my phone was perfect. These are good uses of the remote desktop software, personal use from someone's devices to their PC. There is also chrome remote assistance, which is designed for short case support, passwords, minor troubleshooting.
