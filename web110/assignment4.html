View email message headers. The Google Admin Toolbox MessageHeader tool for checking e-mails for suspicion is a brand-new piece for my toolbox! It is very cool that there is a way to get the info quickly through their own client if you're using Gmail. Many times scammers will spoof an email address to look similar and it's easy to catch, but people make mistakes, someone can slip up or maybe they're just tired and didn't read the address fullyh or weren't thinking because it's too early in the morning and they click an infected link. At work my practice is to be suspicious of any e-mail I'm not expecting. I work for a large hospital and healthcare is constantly under attack by cybercriminals, I get bogus e-mails every week and have to report them for phishing. With this Google Admin Toolbox MessageHeader now, I can see just how reliable the link is, or in the cases where I'm just not sure if it's real or not, I can use this to give another form of evidence.
