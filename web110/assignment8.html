Use anti-malware software to scan your system and test malware detection I use bitdefender as my antivirus software on my PC, and windows defender on my laptop as I do less intrusive activities on there. Bitdefender is good for my PC where I do most of my work and my most sensitive files are kept, and it includes a password manager and VPN though I use expressVPN for that purpose. Using the EICAR test file on my laptop, Windows Defender immediately caught it and alerted me to a malicious file being ran, and allowed me to stop it before it could potentially cause any harm. I am satisfied with this experiment. 4. Password managers and MFA As time has marched onward the amount of passwords people have needed for websites and then smartphone apps has increased at an exponential rate. It can be useful to keep your passwords in a secure place like a personal notebook, however it can be easier to let an app like a password manager take care of it for you. There are paid options like 1password and NordPass, and free options like Proton Pass. I use password managers built into Firefox and Google which are free and suit my needs, and even have automated alerts in case they detect your passwords have been exposed on the web. MFA or 2FA as I knew it most of my life is invaluable as well. I've been saved many times from infiltration attempts because a hacker failed the 2FA, waking up to see dozens of messages with one time passwords requested by somebody else while I was sleeping. Authenticator apps are more secure than 2FA methods like text message codes, as sim cards can be spoofed if someone is determined enough. The same can probably be true for authenticators but they seem more tough to crack. 5. Test your firewall using a testing service such as Gibson Research: ShieldsUP! I did not know about UPnP before running this test, and am pleased to report that my router passed all tests performed. I remember a time when routers were all unsecured and you could just connect to any public network in range, and if you knew what to do you could do some serious damage from the inside (but most of the time it was funny to change the names of their networks :p) My computer performed well against the file sharing security tests, but had a weakness found is it replies to the ping tests. I am happy with the security of my network.
