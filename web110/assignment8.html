Ryne McCormick 4. Password managers and MFA As time has marched onward the amount of passwords people have needed for websites and then smartphone apps has increased at an exponential rate. It can be useful to keep your passwords in a secure place like a personal notebook, however it can be easier to let an app like a password manager take care of it for you. There are paid options like 1password and NordPass, and free options like Proton Pass. I use password managers built into Firefox and Google which are free 5.
